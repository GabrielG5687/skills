# CTA Best Practices

## Button Copy
| Avoid | Use Instead | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Submit` | `Send message` / `Get my result` | Tells user outcome |
| `Click here` | Action-based verb | Meaningless out of context |
| `Sign up` (unclear) | `Create free account` | Sets expectation |
| `Learn more` as primary | Keep as text/link only | Usually low commitment, rarely primary |

## Sizing & States
- **Padding**: Generous (e.g. `px-6 py-3` / 16-24px vertical)
- **Font weight**: 600-700
- **Border radius**: Match overall design, avoid pill unless consistent
- **Focus/hover/active**: Must be distinguishable
- **Disabled**: Clearly indicate non-actionable

## Placement Rules
- **Above fold**: Primary CTA visible without scroll
- **After proof**: Great spot for conversion
- **Don't bury**: Avoid in dropdowns, mega-menus for landing CTAs
- **One hierarchy**: Only ONE `.btn-primary` per section ideally

## Mobile
- **Full width on small screens**: Often better for thumbs
- **Thumb-friendly**: Centered or easy to reach
- **Avoid sticky bars with 3+ actions**
