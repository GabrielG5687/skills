# Social Proof & Trust

## Types
| Type | Impact | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Logos** | Low-Mid | Great for B2B. Use real, recognizable ones. |
| **Testimonials** | High | Include name, role/company when possible. |
| **Numbers/stats** | High | "10k+ users", "98% satisfaction" only if true. |
| **Ratings** | Mid-High | Works well for tools/products. |
| **Case snippets** | High | Short quote + result > long paragraph. |

## Rules
- **Be specific**: "Saved 5h/week" > "Great product"
- **Avoid carousel spam**: Static is often better
- **Don't overdo**: 2-4 strong pieces > 12 generic
- **Match audience**: Show proof your ICP relates to
- **Keep above/beside decision point**: Near CTA or after benefits
